Our Philosophy

Not a madrassa.
Your family's madrassa.

"We built Happy Madrassa for parents who left Kerala, but never wish their children to leave Islam."

Every year, thousands of Malayali Muslim families settle far from home — in Toronto, Melbourne, London, Suva — and discover there is no madrassa on the corner, no Ustaad down the street, no evening class after Maghrib. The rigour of a Kerala religious education becomes a memory rather than an inheritance.

Happy Madrassa exists to close that distance. We pair each child with a single, personally vetted Ustaad for focused, private instruction — conducted with the warmth of a family tutor and the discipline of a traditional Kerala madrassa, scheduled entirely around your household's life abroad.
